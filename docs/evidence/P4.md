## 7. Honest limitations

- **qwen3:4b is unstable on this GPU.** It succeeds in roughly two of three calls at
  `num_ctx=2048`; above 2048 it kills llama-server. The engine retries once after a model
  switch, then falls back to `qwen3:1.7b` and records it. Plans produced through the
  fallback are 1.7B plans.
- **One generation at a time.** 3.3 GiB of usable VRAM admits exactly one resident model;
  concurrency is fixed at 1 (ADR-16, ADR-17).
- **A 5-task objective takes ~2.5 minutes** on this hardware, mostly model switching and
  the 45 s leader call. That is the measured cost, not a target.
- **Approvals are intent-only in Phase 4.** `human_approvals: intent_only` in `/version`.
  Nothing is published; publishing is Phase 7.
- **The chief often picks one department.** It is instructed to pick the departments that
  must contribute; with a 1.7B fallback it frequently names a mission text instead of a
  department id, and the engine's recorded fallback to `strategic_intelligence` handles it
  rather than failing the objective. A 4B plan names departments more reliably.
- **Tools are chosen by the engine, not the model.** An agent cannot discover a tool
  mid-task; this is intentional (docs/TOOL_SECURITY.md).
